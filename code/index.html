Destiny The Game

A beginner's guide to the universe of Destiny.

What is Destiny?

Destiny is an online action game, set in a post-apcocalyptic future, where you play as a Guardian protecting the Sol System from alien enemies.

Character Classes

You can choose one of three classes to play:

Titan

Strong tank-like class that act as the main defense for the team.

Hunter

Fast and agile fighters who excel at DPS and debuffs.

Warlock

Space wizards who use powerful energy to heal, attack, and buff

Things to Do

Here are some of the main game modes:

  • Story Missions and strikes - Play through co-op missions to discover the world of Destiny and gain loot
  • Crucible, Trials of Osiris, and Gambit - Fight against other players in multiplayer matches.
  • Raids and Dungeons - The pinnacle of the Destiny experience. Party up with your friends to solve puzzles and take on powerful enemies for the best weapons and armor in the game.
